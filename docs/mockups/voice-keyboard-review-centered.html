idiolect — review in the centre of the screen, edited with your own keyboard

New direction: when 👁 review is on, stopping a take opens a centred “Review dictation” card over the app — and your normal keyboard (whatever you use) comes up to edit it, exactly like editing any other field. idiolect does not render its own cramped QWERTY for this. On Insert, the approved text drops into the field and the raw→edited pair is still recorded for training.
(Right: the old idea — a small dialog squeezed above idiolect’s keys — for contrast.)
