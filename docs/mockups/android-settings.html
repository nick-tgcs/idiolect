idiolect — Settings, reached by the ⚙ on the mic

Configuration gets its own home: a ⚙ cog joins the mic strip (⌨ · 👁 · 🌐 · ⚙), opening a full Settings screen — so pairing, the model, dictation and learning are reachable any time, not buried in first-run. Connect to PC is the hero: scan once to pair (the QR carries url + code + cert pin); when paired it shows the endpoint and the pinned fingerprint to eyeball against the PC, with Re-pair / Unpair. Manual entry stays as the cleartext (`--no-tls`) fallback.
